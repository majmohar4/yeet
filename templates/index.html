Privacy, safety and retention

Stored

  • The files, folders and clipboard items you upload
  • Your IP address, for rate limits and abuse handling
  • Upload time, size, name and type
  • A random browser ID in local storage, so you can delete your own uploads

Not stored

  • No accounts, names or email addresses
  • No analytics, ads or third-party scripts
  • Passwords are kept only as bcrypt hashes

Safety

  • Every upload is scanned by ClamAV before it gets a link
  • HTML, SVG and scripts are shown as plain text, never run
  • Executables are download-only and marked as such

Retention

  • Files are removed at the expiry you choose (1 hour to 7 days), then purged 24 hours later
  • Single-download files are erased the moment they are downloaded
  • Audit logs are kept 90 days
